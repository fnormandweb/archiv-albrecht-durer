I'm a professional Lead Architect.

With over 12 years of experience, Martin Brown leads our design vision with a focus on sustainable, functional and human-centered spaces.

Her award winning residential projects in the U.S. and Europe reflect an innovative take on modern living.

For Martin, architecture is about shaping experiences—not just buildings. She balances contemporary aesthetics with timeless design, guiding each project from concept to completion with clarity and purpose.

  • 12+ years in international architecture

  • Sustainable, human-focused design expert

  • Skilled in leading cross-functional teams

  • Experience
  • Education
  • Awards

Martin has worked on a diverse range of architectural projects across the U.S. and Europe, from private residences to large-scale housing developments.

As Lead Architect, she has overseen concept development, design execution, and client coordination in award-winning projects known for their sustainability and user-focused design.

Martin holds a Master’s degree in Architecture from the University of California, Berkeley, and a Bachelor’s degree from the University of Edinburgh. Her academic background combines technical precision with a strong foundation in design theory.

  • AIA Residential Design Honor Award – USA (2021)

  • European Architecture Prize – Housing Category (2020)

  • Sustainable Living Design Recognition – Green Build Europe (2019)

Ornava designs timeless, functional spaces with aesthetic clarity and material harmony.
